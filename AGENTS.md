# BuildOpt research navigation

Before selecting research or resuming an experiment, read
[research status](docs/research-status.md) and the
[current execution tracker](docs/plans/buildopt-product-viability-v1-tracker.md).
`BUILDOPT-VIABILITY-V1` is the current program; older plans and handoffs are
historical. Retained code, unchecked boxes and old successor instructions do
not reopen retired routes. Preserve their evidence and terminal decisions.

Use the register to distinguish retired research from retained implementation
and deferred infrastructure. Follow the user's current scope and authorization;
these navigation instructions grant no new experiment, budget or publication.
