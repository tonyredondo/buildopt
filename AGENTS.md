# BuildOpt research navigation

Before selecting research or resuming an experiment, read the
[governing execution plan](docs/plans/buildopt-research-execution-plan-2026-09-14.md),
its [step tracker](docs/plans/buildopt-research-execution-plan-2026-09-14.md#execution-tracker),
and the [research status register](docs/research-status.md).
`BUILDOPT-VIABILITY-V1` remains the current program. The 2026-09-14 plan owns
priorities and sequencing: sustained fixed-correction savings, a usable manual
MVP, then separately proven adaptive value.

The earlier BV plan/tracker and handoffs retain historical evidence; their
conflicting next-step and commercial instructions are superseded. The replay
contract's technical criteria and frozen experiment contracts remain binding
within their scope. Preserve thresholds, protected history and terminal results.

Build Impact is limited to the plan's BO-04 admission review. Retained code,
unchecked boxes and old successor instructions do not reopen generic plan reuse,
adaptive fragments, runtime sweeps or general cache research. Update the governing
plan's tracker and the research status register when evidence changes; do not
create a competing current plan without an explicit supersession record.

Use the register to distinguish retired research from retained implementation
and deferred infrastructure. Follow the user's current scope and authorization;
these navigation instructions grant no new experiment, budget or publication.
