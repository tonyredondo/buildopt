# Wrapper-Coordinated Native Corrections v1

## Decision

`WRAPPER_COORDINATED_NATIVE_CORRECTIONS_V1` tests whether the committed
`buildoptw` can remain a neutral native-Gradle entrypoint while an optional
owner-operated backend combines bounded observations, coordinates one isolated
validator, and delivers evidence-bound native source corrections for owner
review.

The correction is the product-value unit. BuildOpt does not replace Gradle,
run inside the corrected task after adoption, apply source automatically, or
merge a proposal. Backend loss, incomplete evidence, or an untrusted runner
retains native Gradle.

The detailed execution contract is the
[WCNCP tracker](../docs/plans/wrapper-coordinated-native-corrections-poc.md).
The [machine contract](./poc-wrapper-coordinated-native-corrections-v1.json)
freezes its gates and the
[subject policy](./poc-wrapper-coordinated-native-corrections-v1.subjects.json)
freezes selection before the exact ten-family cohort is named.

## Ordered blocks

1. `WCNCP-000` freezes this contract, schemas, subject policy, threat model,
   budgets, evidence boundary, and independent checker without running Gradle.
2. `WCNCP-001..007` implement and prove typed state, HTTPS authority, wrapper
   observation, aggregation/detection, validation coordination, review
   delivery, and three-runner convergence.
3. `WCNCP-008` freezes ten exact public subjects before the first observation
   and captures three ordinary wrapper requests per family from empty state.
4. `WCNCP-009` requires 10/10 conclusive families and at least 3/10 actionable
   material correction families before a candidate build.
5. `WCNCP-010` admits at most one candidate per family and requires at least two
   correctness-qualified families with exact outputs and zero product failures.
6. `WCNCP-011` measures eight balanced pairs only on the controlled runner and
   requires at least two value-qualified families, including one family with no
   prior qualified reviewed-native recipe.
7. `WCNCP-012` measures first-exposure owner review without applying source.
8. `WCNCP-013` independently reconstructs one fixed terminal decision.

No later block borrows authority from an earlier classification result.

## Data and state boundary

The existing owner-operated HTTPS service may share physical content-addressed
bytes between two logical planes:

- Gradle owns opaque cache entries and ordinary eviction/miss behavior.
- BuildOpt owns typed observations, opportunities, proposals, validations,
  decisions, checkpoints, and reconstructable projections.

The planes have different routes, metadata, authorization, retention, and
visibility. A Gradle key cannot address BuildOpt state and blob presence is
never action authority.

WCNCP defines five immutable record schemas:

- `WCNCP_OBSERVATION`;
- `WCNCP_OPPORTUNITY`;
- `WCNCP_PROPOSAL`;
- `WCNCP_VALIDATION`; and
- `WCNCP_DECISION`.

Mutable heads and publication CAS continue to use the existing central-state
contracts. `WCNCP-001` owns persistence and lifecycle implementation; these
schemas alone expose no server route and authorize no action.

## Evidence boundary

Micronaut, Spring, and Elasticsearch recipes may traverse the future system as
`HISTORICAL_RECIPE_SYSTEM_FIXTURE`. Their historical source rows, timing, and
economics cannot contribute to prospective breadth, value, or the terminal
decision.

Prospective evidence must postdate the committed contract and later package,
exact-subject, environment, cache, and state freeze. DNO, NAC, RNPP, EGNP, SWL,
AF, Build Impact, cache-locality, and every other pre-freeze BuildOpt artifact
are forbidden inputs. Public Git and native Gradle diagnostics remain source
material only when freshly captured by the frozen package.

## Environment classes

- `CONTROLLED_PERFORMANCE` owns materiality, overhead, paired value, tails,
  confidence, and payback decisions.
- `STANDARD_HOSTED_CI` owns deterministic compilation, schema, correctness,
  security, portability, and reconstruction gates. All time is diagnostic.
- `LOCAL_FUNCTIONAL` owns focused reproduction only.

An environment class is immutable after execution. Hosted-CI noise is retained
as typed infrastructure evidence and can neither qualify nor reject value.
Missing or unstable controlled capacity yields
`INCOMPLETE_PERFORMANCE_ENVIRONMENT`, not a weaker threshold.

## Threat model

The POC assumes that repository owners and the controlled validator credential
are trusted for their explicit scopes. It does not trust developer machines,
forks, pull requests, cache blobs, uploaded summaries, local absolute paths,
replayed requests, stale heads, or a validator result whose lease expired.

The contract must resist:

- a Gradle cache object being treated as control-state authority;
- cross-repository or cross-kind blob visibility;
- duplicate, reordered, partial, stale, skipped-generation, or conflicting
  publications;
- two validators claiming the same expensive proposal;
- a late validator publishing after lease loss;
- a fork or generic state writer accepting a proposal;
- observation upload leaking credentials, environment values, source archives,
  unsafe arguments, usernames, or machine paths;
- a proposal applying against a drifted source preimage;
- owner acceptance being reported as source application or merge;
- hosted-CI timing entering controlled value arithmetic; and
- historical diagnostic success contaminating the prospective result.

Mitigations are immutable typed records, canonical SHA-256 identities,
idempotency, generation CAS, exact proposal leases, actor-scoped authority,
allowlisted observations, local redaction, source preimage/postimage binding,
exact inverse patches, and independent reconstruction.

## Frozen gates and budgets

- Ten primary public families and two frozen reserve revisions per family.
- Three ordinary wrapper observations and at most two additional native
  diagnostics per family.
- At most one proposal per family and one concurrent controlled validation.
- At least 500 ms and 2% controlled critical-path materiality.
- At least five correctness starts before timing.
- Eight balanced pairs and 8/8 favorable deltas for qualification.
- Positive mean and conservative paired 95% interval, non-regressive p95,
  exact outputs, zero product failures, and at most 300-build customer payback.
- Twelve controlled-machine hours, 30 GiB concurrent experiment footprint,
  and 10 GiB free-space reserve above the next declared footprint.
- One hundred milliseconds post-child upload deadline and a local outbox
  bounded to 100 MiB, 1,000 items, or 30 days.

Only `CONTROLLED_PERFORMANCE` rows evaluate numerical gates. Hosted CI may use
smaller fixtures and generous timeouts for deterministic control-flow proof,
but its timings are `DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY`.

## WCNCP-000 acceptance

This planning block passes only when:

- the human plan, this specification, machine contract, and subject policy
  agree on blocks, gates, decisions, budgets, environment classes, and
  authority;
- all five Draft 2020-12 schemas compile;
- a valid and invalid vector for each record is independently checked;
- the ten subject slots remain unselected and no prospective observation,
  candidate, build, timing, review, or value claim exists;
- the evidence index and repository documentation identify `WCNCP-001` as the
  next block; and
- focused plan/schema, layout, documentation, tracker, ShellCheck, and static
  Base CI integration checks pass.

## POC boundary

Passing `WCNCP-000` authorizes only the separately requested implementation of
`WCNCP-001`. It does not authorize public source changes, prospective Gradle
execution, a proposal, review, production, automatic apply/merge, Test
Optimization, soak, design partners, billing, hosted SaaS, or a speedup claim.
