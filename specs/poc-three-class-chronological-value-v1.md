# Three-Class Chronological Value v1

`THREE_CLASS_CHRONOLOGICAL_VALUE_V1` tests whether the three exact public
classes that independently passed robust installed-path value also produce net
value across ordinary later commits. It requalifies each class with the same
executable, requires 8/8 positive calibration pairs and portable native outputs,
then compares persistent optimized-native control and BuildOpt candidate arms
on the three immediate first-parent descendants of the qualified revision.

The descendant selection is mechanical and frozen before execution. Every
learning, publication, selection, synchronization, materialization and fallback
cost is charged. Every selected arm must reproduce the complete required-output
inventory and SHA-256 exactly; every fallback must execute optimized native
Gradle. A subject passes only with at least one selected replay and non-negative
cumulative net value after its charged cost.

The terminal bounded-product gate requires all three qualification decisions,
8/8 and portability for every observed subject, all nine descendant observations,
zero product failures, at least two of three subjects paid back, and positive
aggregate cumulative net value. A failure is retained as evidence and stops the
claim. Percentages are not averaged across repositories. This remains a POC for
these three exact classes; it does not authorize production, generic breadth,
soak, design-partner claims or Test Optimization.
