# Reviewed Native Patch Portfolio v1

`REVIEWED_NATIVE_PATCH_PORTFOLIO_V1` changes the product unit from a dynamic
cross-commit optimizer decision to one independently reviewed, validated and
reversible native Gradle source correction. After acceptance, Gradle is the
only runtime on the customer build path.

The pivot may select only normalization-aware candidates that already have a
complete public correctness result. That predecessor result is selection
evidence, not fresh correctness or value evidence. Every selected proposal
must reproduce its own control/candidate correctness from empty experiment
state before it can receive timing rows. A failed, incomplete or uneconomic
proposal is rejected independently and cannot invalidate or validate another
proposal.

The fixed initial portfolio contains the four completed NAC v2 classes:

- Micronaut `PythonVfsBytecodeCompile` with reviewed relative-path semantics;
- OpenTelemetry `GenerateInstrumentationVersionClassTask`;
- Spring `ArchitectureCheck`; and
- Spring `ShadowSource`.

Repository and task names are evidence labels only. Product admission remains
the versioned source classifier, explicit normalization facts, digest-bound
patch transaction, owner review and proposal-local proof.

## Gates

Each proposal must independently provide exact complete outputs, required
invalidation, cross-root restoration, native fallback, exact revert and zero
product failures. A passing proposal then receives one excluded stabilization
and eight balanced control/candidate pairs against optimized native Gradle.
Qualification requires 8/8 positive pairs, at least 500 ms and 2% mean saving,
a positive paired 95% interval, non-regressive p95 and finite payback including
measured generation, validation and delivery costs. Human review time is
reported separately and may not be invented.

The portfolio POC requires at least two qualifying proposals across at least
two public families, positive signed measured portfolio value, exact outputs
and zero product failures. This threshold belongs to the new proposal product;
it does not reinterpret or reopen the closed NAC every-candidate gate.

## Budget and boundaries

The campaign permits at most four proposals, six correctness Gradle starts per
proposal and seventeen value invocations per correctness-qualified proposal.
It may consume at most 20 GiB of additional disk. The runner stops before the
next proposal when a proposal fails correctness or when the remaining disk is
below 5 GiB. Public checkouts are disposable and patches are reverted exactly.

No automatic merge, production promotion, soak, design-partner work, Test
Optimization behavior, repository-name rule or dynamic BuildOpt candidate path
is authorized.
