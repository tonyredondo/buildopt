# Spring Messaging Fresh Graph Confirmation v1

`SPRING_MESSAGING_FRESH_GRAPH_CONFIRMATION_V1` performs one untimed
optimized-native `testClasses` request at the newest exact member of the
product-window selected group. PWGR's 11 source matches select the subject but
cannot supply a result.

The request must independently confirm owner `:spring-messaging`, family
`DEPENDENCY_SOURCE`, at least five compatible commits inside the product's
64-commit horizon, at least one omitted project, complete output closure, exact
required outputs and zero product failures. The raw graph and manifest remain
part of the captured state.

One Gradle start is allowed, bounded to 30 minutes and 12 workers. Candidate
execution, calibration, timing samples, patches, production and Test
Optimization are forbidden. A pass authorizes only a separately frozen
candidate-correctness contract; any mismatch stops the route.
