# Producer-Bound Lifetime Generalization Protocol

## Purpose

This block tests whether producer-atomic volatility quarantine and the
cross-commit lifetime path transfer unchanged from Spring Framework to a
second non-Kafka Gradle family. It is a bounded proof-of-concept experiment,
not a production activation policy.

Micronaut Core is the frozen subject because its earlier `assemble`
qualification saved 5,422.625 ms or 13.67% with eight of eight positive pairs,
but one native JAR differed across independent roots and stopped the lifetime
experiment before any descendant timing. That is the exact portability
failure the generic quarantine mechanism is intended to address.

## Frozen evidence boundary

The qualification parent, target and three public first-parent descendants
are copied unchanged from `poc-qualified-lifetime-v2.json`, where they were
registered before this experiment. No descendant may be added, removed or
reordered after timing begins.

The run uses the same automatic POC policy as the completed Spring replay:
eight alternating pairs, at least six positive pairs, a strictly positive
deterministic lower bound, non-regressive candidate p95, at least 500 ms and
2% mean saving, exact fallback and payback within 30 matching builds.

## Portability and lifetime decisions

Two independent native observations bind complete output paths to Gradle
producers. If one output differs, every output of that producer is quarantined
and rebuilt locally. Only stable output inventories with exact SHA-256 values
may be transported.

Each frozen descendant is evaluated in order. A structurally and economically
compatible revision may select the profile; any incompatible revision must
retain optimized native Gradle before execution. Selected value, native arm
variation, qualification cost and cumulative economics remain separate.

The mechanism broadens beyond Spring only if at least one Micronaut descendant
selects the unchanged profile with positive attributable saving, exact stable
outputs, the complete quarantined path set and zero product-attributable
failures. A safe native-only result is valid evidence but does not broaden the
claim.

## Boundaries

This protocol adds no repository-name rule to product code, does not average
repository percentages, does not add mechanism percentages, does not require
soak or a design partner, does not authorize production use and does not
include Test Optimization.
