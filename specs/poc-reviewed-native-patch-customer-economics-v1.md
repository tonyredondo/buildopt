# Reviewed Native Patch Customer Economics v1

`REVIEWED_NATIVE_PATCH_CUSTOMER_ECONOMICS_V1` tests the next missing POC
boundary after owner acceptance: whether the unchanged normalization-aware
source detector can find, validate and deliver a useful correction in a third
public Gradle family with first-exposure owner review included in payback.

The experiment starts from five exact public revisions that are outside the
NAC/RNPP cohort: Ktor, Hibernate ORM, Spotless, Mockito and SpotBugs. Earlier
BuildOpt results may supply only repository URLs, revisions, toolchain facts
and workflow compatibility. They may not supply classifier rows, candidates,
correctness outcomes, durations or decisions. Every result row is fresh.

## Ordered gates

1. A v2 source-only audit must be conclusive for all five families and expose
   at least one marker-only or reviewed-relative action family. Repository and
   task names are labels only.
2. At most two source-ranked proposals from at most two families may receive
   fresh patch compilation and proposal-local correctness. Each proposal must
   preserve complete outputs across roots, exercise required invalidation,
   restore from native Gradle cache, revert exactly and add zero product
   failures.
3. Each correctness-qualified proposal receives one excluded stabilization
   and eight balanced optimized-native/candidate pairs. Qualification requires
   8/8 positive pairs, at least 500 ms and 2% mean saving, a positive paired
   95% interval, non-regressive p95 and finite machine payback.
4. Only a value-qualified proposal may be delivered as a controlled fork-local
   draft. The owner must see it for the first time, report active review seconds,
   understand it and accept it with no more than one proposal clarification.
5. The terminal POC passes only when a third public family qualifies and the
   conservative combined payback, including measured machine work plus owner
   active-review milliseconds, is at most 300 compatible builds.

Active review time is owner-reported. Chat timestamps, total elapsed session
time and agent inference are forbidden substitutes. Human and machine time are
also reported separately so the combined elapsed-time-equivalent projection
cannot be mistaken for a labor-cost model.

## Budget and stop conditions

The source audit may inspect exactly five revisions. Public execution is
limited to two proposals, six correctness Gradle starts per proposal and
seventeen value invocations per qualified proposal. Additional experiment
storage is capped at 8 GiB. No public build starts when free disk is below
8 GiB, and the route stops before another start when it falls below 6 GiB.

A failed source, correctness, value, review or payback prerequisite stops its
dependent work honestly. Thresholds do not move after observation. The route
does not authorize an upstream pull request, merge, default-branch mutation,
automatic application, production, soak, design-partner work or Test
Optimization behavior.
