# Prospective Reviewed Native Patch Controlled Trial v1

## Purpose

Test whether the reviewed-native-patch product finds and delivers new value on
a frozen holdout cohort without selecting repositories, candidates, thresholds
or controls after source results are known.

## Frozen sequence

1. Resolve and freeze ten previously unused public Gradle repository revisions.
2. Reconstruct normalization-aware source classifications from those revisions
   without consuming earlier opportunity reports.
3. Bind source actions to real owner workflows and admit diagnostics only for
   bounded tasks that could clear 500 ms, 2% and 300-build economics.
4. Prove exact output, restoration, invalidation and revert correctness only for
   proposals that pass native critical-path diagnostics.
5. Measure value with one excluded stabilization per absolute root and eight
   balanced pairs against optimized native Gradle.
6. Expose only value-qualified proposals to the owner, measure active review
   time, and exercise digest-bound isolated apply/revert delivery.
7. Charge every holdout discovery, diagnostic, correctness, value, delivery and
   owner-review cost before the terminal decision.

## Gates

- Source: 10/10 conclusive and at least one action family.
- Diagnostics: at most six starts across at most three families; selected work
  must be on the native critical path and address at least 500 ms and 2%.
- Correctness: at most six Gradle starts per proposal, complete exact outputs,
  same-root and cross-root restoration, required invalidation, exact source
  revert and zero product failures.
- Value: eight of eight positive pairs, at least 500 ms and 2% mean saving,
  positive paired 95% interval and non-regressive candidate p95.
- Review: first exposure only after value qualification, at most 15 active
  minutes and at most one proposal clarification.
- Terminal: at least one new qualified public family and combined payback no
  greater than 300 compatible builds.

## Boundaries

The trial may use clean temporary public checkouts and owner-controlled isolated
delivery state. It may not open an upstream pull request, mutate a public
default branch, deploy production behavior, apply or merge automatically,
perform a soak, claim arbitrary Gradle coverage or modify Test Optimization.
